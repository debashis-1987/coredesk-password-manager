Privacy Policy

Coredesk Password Manager · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary: the app stores your data on your device only. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no trackers, and it never communicates with any server operated by the developer. Your passwords never leave your computer except in files you explicitly create yourself (encrypted backups and mobile exports you choose to save).

What the app stores, and where

Your saved credentials (passwords, cards, notes, 2FA secrets) An encrypted file (vault.bin) on your device
Preferences (theme, window size) A small config file on your device
Backups and mobile exports Files you choose to create, wherever you choose to save them

The vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the encryption key is derived from your master passphrase via PBKDF2 and is never written to disk.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your data, usage statistics, crash reports, or any other information to any server.
  • It does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not require an account and offers no cloud sync.
  • It accesses the network only when you click a link in an entry (your own browser opens that website), and when the Microsoft Store delivers app updates (handled by Windows, not the app).

Optional features and their privacy impact

  • FIDO hardware-key unlock — communicates only with your own USB security key through the operating system's FIDO APIs. No data leaves the device.
  • Key file / quick PIN / auto-lock — purely local mechanisms.
  • Encrypted backup / KeePass (.kdbx) mobile export — files created only when you request them, saved where you choose. What you do with those files (e.g. uploading them to your own cloud storage) is your decision and outside this app.
  • Imports (browser CSV, KeePass, Bitwarden) — read from files you select; nothing is transmitted.

Permissions used

  • runFullTrust (Microsoft Store packaging) — the standard permission for desktop applications; required to run as a normal Windows program.
  • The app requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or other sensitive device permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: contact the developer through the app's Microsoft Store listing (Developer information section).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the next app update and be published on this page.
